## 📝 Customization Guide

### Change Colors
Find and replace in all files:
- `#00629B` → Your primary color
- `#004A7C` → Your primary dark color

### Update Images
Replace placeholder URLs:
- `https://via.placeholder.com/...` → Your image URLs

### Add Content
- **Events**: Edit `eventsData` in script.js
- **Testimonials**: Edit `testimonialsData` in script.js
- **Spotlight**: Edit `spotlightData` in script.js
- **Team Members**: Copy/paste HTML blocks in index.html
- **Courses**: Add course cards in index.html
