# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by and for a student community. Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the website.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give actionable review comments.
- Credit people accurately for their work.
- Handle student information and images responsibly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.
- Misrepresenting branch events, roles, or approvals.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the branch maintainers or faculty advisor. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
