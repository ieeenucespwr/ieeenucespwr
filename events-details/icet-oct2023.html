ICET 2023: International Conference on Emerging Technologies

📅 Date: October 12-13, 2023
⏰ Duration: 16 Hours (2 Days)
👥 Attendees: 500+ Attendees
🎓 Batches: International

Event Overview

The International Conference on Emerging Technologies (ICET) 2023 was a prestigious two-day academic conference bringing together researchers, industry professionals, and students from around the world. Hosted by FAST Peshawar with IEEE support, the conference featured 14 technical sessions covering breakthrough research and innovations.

ICET 2023 provided a platform for presenting cutting-edge research, fostering academic collaborations, and discussing the future of technology across multiple domains including AI, IoT, blockchain, renewable energy, and more.

Key Topics Covered

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Deep learning, NLP, computer vision applications
  • Internet of Things: Smart devices, sensor networks, and connected systems
  • Blockchain Technology: Cryptocurrency, smart contracts, and decentralized applications
  • Renewable Energy: Solar systems, energy optimization, and sustainable solutions
  • Cybersecurity: Network security, cryptography, and threat detection
  • Software Engineering: Modern development practices and software architecture
  • Data Science: Big data analytics, visualization, and predictive modeling

Session Highlights

The conference featured:

  1. 14 technical sessions with 50+ research paper presentations
  2. Keynote speeches from distinguished researchers and industry leaders
  3. Panel discussions on future technology trends and challenges
  4. Poster sessions showcasing innovative research projects
  5. Networking sessions facilitating academic and industry collaborations
  6. Best paper awards recognizing outstanding research contributions

ICET 2023 successfully advanced academic discourse, showcased groundbreaking research, and created lasting connections between academia and industry. The conference proceedings were published in IEEE Xplore, providing global visibility to presented research.
