Mastering LinkedIn

📅 Date: August 21, 2024
⏰ Duration: 1 Hour (CPD)
👥 Attendees: 80+ Students
🎓 Batches: 2021-2024

Event Overview

The "Mastering LinkedIn" workshop was a practical, hands-on session designed to help students maximize their LinkedIn presence for career success. With the growing importance of professional networking platforms, this workshop provided essential skills for leveraging LinkedIn effectively.

Participants learned how to create compelling profiles, engage with professional content, and utilize LinkedIn's powerful job search and networking features to advance their careers.

Key Topics Covered

  • Profile Optimization: Crafting attention-grabbing headlines and compelling summary sections
  • Experience Showcase: Presenting academic projects, internships, and skills effectively
  • Content Strategy: Creating and sharing posts that demonstrate expertise
  • Networking Best Practices: Connection requests, messages, and relationship building
  • Job Search Tools: Using LinkedIn's job board, Easy Apply, and recruiter features
  • Recommendations & Endorsements: Building credibility through peer validation
  • LinkedIn Learning: Accessing courses for skill development

Session Highlights

  1. Live profile reviews and optimization demonstrations
  2. Real-world examples of successful student LinkedIn profiles
  3. Interactive exercises on connection message crafting
  4. Tips from professionals on what recruiters look for
  5. Q&A session addressing specific LinkedIn challenges

Many students left with actionable improvements to their profiles and increased confidence in using LinkedIn for professional networking and job hunting.
